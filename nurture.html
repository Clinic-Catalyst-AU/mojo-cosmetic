What happens after someone takes the skin check

From "my skin has changed" to a booked consultation

Ten days of short, helpful messages from Dr Maria, written for the woman who has noticed her skin changing and doesn't want to look done. Each one teaches something and ends with an easy way to book. Then she stays on a gentle monthly newsletter, because someone not ready today may be ready in three months.

In Go High Level, the moment she submits

Tagged, staged and followed up

Tagslead: skin check plus her concern, e.g. concern: thin dull, and her starting point, e.g. start: within
PipelineNew lead, then Contacted, Consultation offered, Booked, Attended, Treatment plan, Treatment started, Long-term care
ReceptionA task for the front desk if she ticks that she'd like a call, or if she replies to any message
Stops when bookedBooking in Fresha moves her to Booked and stops the sequence. Claude checks Fresha bookings through the Data Connector.

Booking links use Mojo's existing Fresha page. Every message carries an unsubscribe link (email) or STOP to opt out (SMS). No time-limited offers or discounts on doctor-led procedures are used anywhere in this sequence.

Why your skin changed "suddenly" in your fortiesPreview: It's rarely as sudden as it feels

Hi {first name},

Perimenopause isn't a single event. It can run for 10 to 15 years, and symptoms can start in the late thirties. During that time, collagen loss and skin thinning speed up. Nutrition, gut health and inflammation can all show in your skin too.

That's why at Mojo we start with what's happening inside, then decide together what, if anything, to do on the outside. You can keep it to skin only, or look at the bigger picture as well. It's your choice.
