Our approach

Skin from within

Hormones, nutrients, gut health and inflammation all show up in your skin. Dr Maria looks at the whole picture first, then at what your face actually needs. Every option, cost and risk is explained before you decide.

01

Your own platelets

A small sample of your own blood is prepared using the Emcyte PurePRP system, a closed-system device that produces a high-concentration platelet preparation. Not all PRP is prepared the same way, and we will show you the difference.

02

Threads that work beneath

Aptos threads are absorbable threads placed in the deeper layers of the skin to lift and prompt your body's own collagen response. Dr Maria trained in Aptos at an advanced level in Tbilisi, Georgia.

03

Skin that keeps up

SQT, skin needling, LED and our new buccal facial massage keep your skin in rhythm between doctor treatments, with our skin therapist guiding every visit.

Our signature treatment

Not all PRP is prepared the same way

If you're researching PRP you'll come across three different terms. They describe genuinely different preparations, and it's worth knowing which one you're being offered.

PRP

Standard PRP

Typically prepared in a glass test tube.

Emcyte

Emcyte PurePRP

Prepared in an FDA-cleared closed-system device, producing a high-concentration platelet preparation. This is what we use at Mojo.

PRF

Platelet rich fibrin

A different preparation again, with its own process and uses.
