Our approach

Regeneration first. Always.

We would rather help your skin rebuild than simply cover it. Every plan starts with a consultation with a doctor, and every option, cost and risk is explained before you decide.

01

Your own platelets

PRP is prepared from a small sample of your own blood. We use a concentrated preparation, and your doctor explains exactly how it is made at your consultation.

02

Threads that work beneath

Aptos threads are placed under the skin by a doctor and are designed to lift while prompting your body to build collagen around them. Not all threads are the same, and we will show you why.

03

Skin that keeps up

SQT, skin needling, LED and our new buccal facial massage keep your skin in rhythm between doctor treatments, with our skin therapist guiding every visit.
