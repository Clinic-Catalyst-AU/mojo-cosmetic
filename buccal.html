What happens in a session

Calm, unhurried and hands on

Buccal Sculpt and Release is a beauty therapy treatment, carried out by Mojo's skin therapist under the clinic's clinical oversight.

1

A quick chat

Your therapist asks how your face feels, about any recent dental work, injectables or skin treatments, and checks the treatment suits you.

2

Outside the face

Massage along the jaw, cheeks, neck and around the eyes to warm up the muscles and ease surface tension.

3

Inside the cheeks

With gloved hands, your therapist works gently from inside the mouth on the cheek muscles, where a lot of tension sits.

4

Rest and aftercare

A few quiet minutes, then simple aftercare tips for the next day or so. Maria to confirm session length.

Like any treatment, it isn't right for everyone. Things such as recent dental work, mouth ulcers or cold sores, jaw problems, recent injectables or facial surgery, and pregnancy are checked first. Questions people ask

Before you book

What does "buccal" mean?

Buccal refers to the cheeks, and the inside of the cheeks in particular. Buccal Sculpt and Release includes gentle work from inside the mouth on the cheek muscles, as well as massage on the outside of the face.

Who does the treatment?

Mojo's skin therapist, under the clinic's clinical oversight. It is a beauty therapy treatment, not a medical procedure.

Does it hurt?

Most people describe it as deep and firm rather than painful, and your therapist adjusts the pressure as you go. Some people feel a little tender afterwards.

How long does it take?

Maria to confirm session length, then this answer is filled in.

Can I have it with my other treatments?

Often, yes, but timing matters around things like injectables, dental work and some skin treatments. Your therapist plans it with you.

Is it right for me?

That's what the check-in before your first session is for. If something means it isn't suitable, your therapist will tell you and suggest another option.
