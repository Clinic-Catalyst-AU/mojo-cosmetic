Quick wins for this week

Things your team can do now

Small changes your head receptionist and IT guy can make straight away, before any bigger system goes in.

01

Give the thread page its own words

Point the Aptos thread lifting page title at Neutral Bay and write it about threads from top to bottom, with the thread guide form at the end. It becomes the landing page for your thread campaigns, on Google or Meta.

02

Put Mojo on the newsletter sign-up

Change the homepage sign-up line to "the latest news and offers from Mojo Cosmetic Clinic" so every new subscriber knows exactly who they have joined.

03

Match Fresha to your leads every two days

Fresha doesn't talk to other systems, so export your client list every couple of days and match it against new leads. From then on you can see which campaign actually booked people in.

04

Film 30 seconds, exactly as you are

No hair and makeup needed. Talk to your phone about one thing you love, like PRP or why not all threads are the same, and send it to Kelly. You'll see how good it looks once it's tidied up.

05

Give the PRP page its own words too

Bring the Emcyte PurePRP explanation from the Mojo Klinik site across to the PRP page on the cosmetic site, so the page, its price and its heading all describe PRP. It becomes your hero treatment page.

06

Ask for a Google review after every visit

An automatic text after each completed appointment, with a direct link to your Google reviews and one gentle follow-up. It brings a steady stream of recent reviews to both clinics.

The system

How a stranger becomes a booked consult

Two stages. First people get to know you, then we invite the ones who are interested. It moves you from "treatment, offer, lead form" to you, then education, then trust, then a consultation.

1. WatchYour teaching videos reach women around Neutral Bay and North Sydney
2. RememberEveryone who watched or engaged goes into a retargeting audience
3. Raise a handAds built around a concern: "my skin has changed", "I don't want filler", thinning hair, laxity, Onda
4. TaggedEach lead lands in Go High Level tagged with their concern, and gets follow-up that matches it
Follow-up

A sequence for every concern

Straight away, a thank-you and Mojo's approach. Day 1, a short video of you. Day 3, education on skin ageing. Day 5, what happens at a consultation. Day 7, common questions. Day 10, an invitation to book. After that, gentle education continues, because someone not ready today may be ready in three months.

Visibility

See every lead through to revenue

New lead, contacted, consultation offered, booked, attended, treatment plan, treatment started, long-term care. Fresha doesn't talk back automatically, so reception updates it from a Fresha report twice a week. For the first time you'll see which campaign actually brought in revenue.

After hours

An assistant that answers at 9:30pm

Trained on your tone, services, booking process and common questions, across web chat and messages. The line is clear: the assistant handles admin and directions, and you handle medicine. Anything about diagnosis, suitability or personal treatment advice goes straight to your team.

For you

Claude runs your Go High Level

You already use Claude. We connect it to your Go High Level account so it builds the forms, follow-ups and campaigns when you ask in plain English. Your head receptionist runs the finished system, which is what she's brilliant at.

Events

The Skin + Hormones Evening

Evenings built around something she wants solved, not around the clinic: "Why your skin changes in your 40s". You talk for 20 to 30 minutes, then take questions. Invitations and reminders go out automatically, and one evening gives you content, leads and referrals.

Your team

Only you do what only you can do

Reception handles enquiries, bookings, reviews and follow-up. Your therapist looks after skin pathways and rebooking, including the new buccal facial. Automation handles first replies and reminders. You keep clinical decisions, regenerative treatments and teaching.

Your first 90 days

Position, build, then scale

DAYS 1 TO 30

Position and fix

Bring both brands under one Mojo philosophy. Put you front and centre. Tidy the cosmetic website page by page. Map the Go High Level pipeline, track leads as far as Fresha allows, switch on review requests and film your first bank of videos.

DAYS 30 TO 60

Build the engine

Launch your teaching videos and retargeting, then the concern-based lead campaigns and their follow-up. Switch on the assistant, segment the database so people only get emails on topics they care about, launch the PRP campaign and build skin and hormone content for Google and AI search.

DAYS 60 TO 90

Scale what works

Measure cost per lead, consultations, attendance, treatments and revenue by campaign. Put more behind whatever brings in revenue. Test tightly controlled Google searches for PRP and regenerative treatments, and run your first Skin + Hormones Evening.

LATER

Your knowledge, as a course

Go High Level can also host your programmes as courses and memberships, turning your knowledge into income that doesn't need another patient in the chair.
